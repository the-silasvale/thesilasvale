The Author

A voice that pulls you into the dark

Silas Vale is a bestselling author of dark psychological thrillers, intense medical dramas, and obsessive dark romances — writing with raw emotional depth and unflinching intensity.

His stories explore power dynamics, fractured identities, forbidden desire, and the dangerous line between love and possession. From the memory-manipulating corridors of Blackwood Institute to the brutal hierarchy of a hospital's night shift, every book asks the same question: what are you willing to surrender?
